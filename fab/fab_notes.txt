NMOS LDO board, rev A: fabrication notes

Board
  54 x 42 mm, 4 layers, 1.6 mm FR4
  35 um copper on all four layers
  Stackup: F.Cu, 0.2104 mm prepreg, In1.Cu, 1.02 mm core, In2.Cu, 0.2104 mm prepreg, B.Cu
  ENIG finish, solder mask and silkscreen on both sides
  Smallest track 0.25 mm, smallest clearance 0.2 mm, smallest drill 0.3 mm

Vias
  Fill and cap the 25 vias inside the Q1 tab pad, IPC-4761 type VII.
  They are the 5 x 5 array of 0.3 mm vias under Q1. Filled vias keep solder
  from wicking down them during reflow and leaving voids under the tab.
  Every other via is tented.
